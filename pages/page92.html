Divisibility
DIVISIBILITY
2Divisible by 2
If the last digit of a number is even or 0, the number is divisible by 2.
5Divisible by 5
If the last digit (the ones place) is 5 or 0, the number is divisible by 5.
10Divisible by 10
If the last digit is 0, the number is divisible by 10.
If it ends in 0 it is divisible by all three — 2, 5 and 10 — e.g. 40, 70.
