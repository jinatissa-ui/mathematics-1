ROUNDING TO THE NEAREST HUNDRED
How to write a number as a multiple of 100 (ending in 00)
1Put 00 in place of the last two digits.
2If the tens digit is 5 or more — add 1 to the hundreds.
3If the tens digit is under 5 — keep the hundreds as it is.
Examples — when the tens digit is 5, the hundred goes up
