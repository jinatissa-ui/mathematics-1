Easier cases
Cut the zeros, then divide
Cut the same number of zeros from the end of both numbers. That leaves an easier division.
2 300 ÷ 100 → 23 ÷ 1 = 23
2 400 ÷ 200 → 24 ÷ 2 = 12
3 600 ÷ 300 → 36 ÷ 3 = 12
4 500 ÷ 500 → 45 ÷ 5 = 9
5 600 ÷ 700 → 56 ÷ 7 = 8
Cutting zeros does not change the answer.
