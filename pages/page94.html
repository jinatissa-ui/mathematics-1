Dividing by 4 and 6
DIVISIBILITY · 4 & 6
Look at the digits and decide quickly whether it divides.
4Dividing by 4
Last 2Look only at the last two digits of the number.
RuleIf those two digits divide by 4, or are 00 → divisible by 4
700, 500, 900 — if it ends in 00 it always divides by 4.
6Dividing by 6
By 2Is the last digit even or 0?
By 3Is the digit sum 3, 6 or 9?
RuleIf it divides by both 2 and 3 → divisible by 6
It must divide by both — one alone is not enough.
4 → last 2 digits  ·  6 → 2 and 3
