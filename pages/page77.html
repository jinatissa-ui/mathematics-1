Let's Estimate
MAKE ESTIMATES

1A primary school has 5 classes, Grades 1 to 5. Each class has 12 children. Estimate the number of children in the school.

12 × 5 ≈ 60

2A banana bunch has 8 large hands. Each hand has 15 fruits. Estimate the number of fruits in the bunch.

15 × 8 ≈ 120

3One 12-foot wall of a room took 240 bricks. The room is 12 ft long and 12 ft wide. How many bricks are needed for the 4 walls? (Do not deduct doors and windows.)

240 × 4 ≈ 960
