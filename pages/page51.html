Hands of the Clock
HANDS OF THE CLOCK
Second hand The fastest hand. One minute passes when it crosses the 60 small divisions on the clock face.
Minute hand The long hand. One hour passes when it goes around once.
Hour hand The big short hand. One day passes when it goes around twice (12 × 2).
1 minute=60 seconds
1 hour=60 minutes
1 day=24 hours
