Parts of an Angle

PARTS OF AN ANGLE

An angle is formed when the ends of two straight line segments are joined together.

Those two straight line segments are called the arms of the angle.

The point where they meet is the vertex of the angle.

Dynamic angles DYNAMIC

The angle changes as the arm turns (rotation).

Static angles STATIC

Angles that do not change, such as the corner of a wall, a table or a plank.
